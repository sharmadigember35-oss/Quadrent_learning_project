Employees receive their salaries on the last working day of each month.

The human resources department handles employee benefits, leave requests, and workplace policies.

Employees are entitled to ten paid sick leave days per year.

The company provides training programs to help employees improve their technical skills.

Employees must inform their managers if they cannot attend work due to illness.

The IT department helps employees resolve computer, software, and network problems.

The company reviews employee performance twice a year.

Interns receive a monthly stipend based on their internship agreement.

Employees must use their official email accounts for work-related communication.
